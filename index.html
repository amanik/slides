Objectives

Why We're Building This

  • User value: Huge impact on all-cause mortality. Key longevity metric, even for active users.
  • Business value: Highly modifiable → high retention. Trackable over time. Stickiness increases with wearables.
  • Clinical value: Independent predictor of mortality. Affects glucose control, cholesterol, blood pressure.

The Metric

Weekly Sedentary Index

  • Ratio of total sedentary hours per day divided by number of breaks taken
  • Lower numbers are better — they indicate regular break-up of sedentary periods
  • Score of 1 = one break per hour sitting. Below 1 = excellent. Above 2.5 = at risk.

Scoring Ranges

RangeScoreMeaning
Optimal0 – 1≥1 break per hour of sedentary time
Normal1 – 2.5Some breaks, but room for improvement
At Risk> 2.5Extended unbroken sedentary periods

How the Score Is Calculated

  • Sum reported sedentary hours across activities for a weekday estimate
  • Sum reported breaks per weekday
  • Divide: hours ÷ breaks = sedentary index
  • Worst possible score: 6. Best possible: 0. Zero breaks → automatic 6.

Placement & Flow

Where the Result Lives

  • Under the Exercise Additional Recommendations page
  • Most recommendations focus on movement — natural home for this metric
  • Visual design: traditional bar/graph result card, similar to biomarker cards

User Flow

  • User completes 2–10 sedentary time questions in the onboarding Jotform
  • Raw results flow into the backend
  • Front-end of internal tool calculates the index — no Excel dependency
  • Same pattern as gut microbiome indices: built for wearable integration

Recommendations

The goal is not just measurement. It's behavior change.

Targeted Markers

  • Metabolic: A1c, HDL-c, LDL-c, Triglycerides, Non-HDL-c, ApoB
  • Cardiovascular: Framingham Risk Score
  • Particularly important for people at risk of diabetes — breaks improve glucose control and insulin resistance

Key Strategies

  • Increase breaks: Aim for a short break every 30 minutes during sedentary activities
  • Reduce screen time: Cut phone and video watching by at least 30 minutes each
  • Active commuting: Standing or brief walking during travel times
  • Stay mindful: Awareness of long periods of inactivity

Personalized Action Plans

  • Activity-level granularity: desk work, commuting, screen time each get tailored suggestions
  • Incremental: choose 1–2 strategies, build consistency over one month
  • Example: 3 hrs desk work with 2 breaks → new plan of 6 breaks (every 30 min)
  • Standing desks, walking phone calls, stretching during shows
